# Code of Conduct
## The Organization — Human Resources Policy Manual, Section 1

### 1.1 Purpose
This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional and ethical
behavior expected of every employee, contractor, and representative of The
Organization, regardless of role or seniority.

### 1.2 Respectful Workplace
The Organization is committed to a workplace free of harassment,
discrimination, and retaliation. Harassment or discrimination on the basis
of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic
information, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any
other legally protected characteristic will not be tolerated and may result
in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 1.3 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside financial interest, employment, or
relationship that could reasonably be perceived to conflict with their
duties. Disclosures are made in writing to the employee's supervisor and
the Ethics Office and are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

### 1.4 Gifts and Business Courtesies
Employees may not accept gifts, meals, or entertainment from vendors,
customers, or business partners with a value exceeding $75 in a calendar
year without prior written approval from the Ethics Office. Cash or
cash-equivalent gifts of any value are prohibited.

### 1.5 Confidential Information
Employees must protect confidential business information, trade secrets,
and any personally identifiable information (PII) they encounter in the
course of their duties, including employee, customer, or constituent data.
Confidential information may not be disclosed outside The Organization
except as required by law or with proper authorization.

### 1.6 Use of Organization Systems
Organization computing systems, including this employee portal, are provided
for business use. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in
communications or data stored on Organization systems. Attempts to access
another employee's account, records, or data without authorization are
strictly prohibited.

### 1.7 Reporting Violations
Employees who become aware of a violation of this Code, or who experience or
witness harassment, discrimination, or unethical conduct, are encouraged to
report it to their supervisor, HR, or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.
Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is strictly
prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action.

### 1.8 Compliance with Law
Employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations relevant to
their role, including data privacy laws, workplace safety regulations, and
any sector-specific compliance requirements applicable to government,
education, or corporate operations.
