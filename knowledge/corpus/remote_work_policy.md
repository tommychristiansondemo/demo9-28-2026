# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
## The Organization — Human Resources Policy Manual, Section 8

### 8.1 Eligibility
Remote and hybrid work arrangements are available to employees whose role
has been designated "remote-eligible" by their department head, subject to
satisfactory performance and at least 90 days of continuous employment in
the current role.

### 8.2 Work Arrangements
Three arrangements are available:
- **On-site**: employee works from an Organization facility full-time.
- **Hybrid**: employee works from an Organization facility a minimum of 2
  days per week, with the remaining days remote, as scheduled with their
  supervisor.
- **Fully Remote**: employee works from an approved remote location full
  time, subject to annual re-approval.

### 8.3 Equipment and Security
The Organization provides a standard equipment set (laptop, monitor,
headset) for approved remote and hybrid employees. All remote work must be
performed on Organization-issued or Organization-managed devices with
full-disk encryption and endpoint security software enabled. Use of public,
unsecured Wi-Fi networks for accessing Organization systems is prohibited;
a company-provided VPN must be used at all times when working outside an
Organization facility.

### 8.4 Working Hours and Availability
Remote and hybrid employees are expected to be available during their
department's designated core hours and to maintain the same responsiveness
standards as on-site employees. Deviations from standard hours must be
approved in advance by a supervisor.

### 8.5 Home Office Stipend
Employees approved for Fully Remote arrangements receive a one-time $500
home office setup stipend and a recurring $50/month connectivity stipend,
both processed through payroll.

### 8.6 Data Handling While Remote
Remote employees handling confidential, PII, or regulated data must follow
the Data Handling and Compliance Policy (Section 9) at all times, including
when working from a home office or approved alternate location. Printing
confidential documents at a non-Organization location is prohibited.

### 8.7 Revocation
Remote work arrangements are a privilege, not an entitlement, and may be
modified or revoked by the department head at any time based on business
need or performance concerns, with reasonable notice except in cases
involving a security or compliance violation.
