# Data Handling and Compliance Policy
## The Organization — Human Resources Policy Manual, Section 9

### 9.1 Purpose
This policy sets minimum standards for handling personally identifiable
information (PII), protected health information (PHI), and other
confidential or regulated data encountered by employees, including data
processed through the internal Policy & Benefits Assistant chatbot and any
other Organization system.

### 9.2 Data Classification
Data handled by the Organization falls into three tiers:
- **Public**: approved for external release (e.g., published policy
  summaries).
- **Internal**: for use within the Organization only (e.g., internal memos,
  aggregate reporting).
- **Restricted**: PII, PHI, financial account numbers, Social Security
  Numbers, and similar regulated data. Restricted data requires the highest
  level of protection under this policy.

### 9.3 Minimum Necessary Principle
Employees and automated systems, including AI assistants and chatbots, must
access and process only the minimum Restricted data necessary to perform a
specific, authorized task. Bulk export or broad querying of Restricted data
without a documented business justification is prohibited.

### 9.4 AI and Automated Systems
Any AI assistant, chatbot, or automated decision system deployed by the
Organization that processes employee or constituent input must apply
automated content filtering and PII redaction/masking safeguards
consistent with this policy before any Restricted data is logged, stored,
or included in a response. Employees should never be required to submit
full Social Security Numbers, full financial account numbers, or other
Restricted identifiers through a chat interface; assistants are expected to
decline or redirect such requests to a secure, dedicated channel.

### 9.5 Retention
Restricted data collected in the course of processing a leave request,
benefits enrollment, or similar transaction is retained only as long as
necessary to complete the transaction and satisfy applicable recordkeeping
requirements, and is not to be retained indefinitely in chat logs or
transcripts.

### 9.6 Incident Reporting
Any suspected exposure, unauthorized access, or mishandling of Restricted
data must be reported immediately to the Information Security Office. Do
not attempt to independently investigate or remediate a suspected data
incident.

### 9.7 Third-Party and Government Data Sharing
Restricted data may be shared with third parties or government agencies
only pursuant to a valid legal basis (contract, subpoena, regulatory
requirement) and with prior review by the Legal and Compliance team.
