# Employee Benefits Handbook
## The Organization — Human Resources Policy Manual, Section 6

### 6.1 Eligibility
All employees regularly scheduled for 30 or more hours per week are eligible
for the benefits described in this section beginning on the first day of the
month following 30 days of continuous employment. Part-time employees under
30 hours per week are eligible only for the retirement savings plan
described in Section 6.5.

### 6.2 Health Insurance
The Organization offers three medical plan tiers: Standard, Preferred, and
High-Deductible with Health Savings Account (HSA). The Organization
contributes 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 60% of the
premium for dependent coverage under all three tiers. Open enrollment occurs
annually in November for coverage effective January 1. Qualifying life
events (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage) permit enrollment
changes outside the open enrollment window within 30 days of the event.

### 6.3 Dental and Vision
Dental and vision coverage are offered as optional add-ons at full employee
cost, available for enrollment during the same open enrollment period as
medical coverage described in Section 6.2.

### 6.4 Life and Disability Insurance
The Organization provides basic life insurance and short-term disability
insurance at no cost to eligible employees, at a coverage amount of one
times annual base salary. Employees may purchase additional voluntary life
insurance up to five times annual salary at group rates through payroll
deduction.

### 6.5 Retirement Savings Plan
All employees, including part-time employees under Section 6.1, are eligible
to contribute to the 401(k) retirement savings plan starting on their first
day of employment. The Organization matches 100% of employee contributions
up to 4% of base salary, and 50% of the next 2% of base salary, for a
maximum possible match of 5% of base salary. Employer matching contributions
vest over a 3-year graded schedule (0% before 1 year, 50% after 1 year, 100%
after 2 years of service).

### 6.6 Tuition Assistance
Employees who have completed 6 months of continuous service may apply for
tuition assistance of up to $5,250 per calendar year for job-related
coursework at an accredited institution, subject to a grade requirement of
"B" or better (or Pass in a pass/fail course) and manager approval prior to
enrollment.

### 6.7 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and their household members have access to a confidential
Employee Assistance Program offering counseling, financial guidance, and
legal referral services at no cost, available 24/7 by phone.

### 6.8 COBRA and Continuation Coverage
Employees who lose eligibility for health coverage due to a qualifying event
(termination, reduction in hours, etc.) may continue coverage under COBRA at
their own expense for up to 18 months, consistent with applicable federal
law. Details are provided in a separate COBRA notice at the time of the
qualifying event.
