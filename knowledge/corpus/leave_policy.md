# Leave and Time-Off Policy
## The Organization — Human Resources Policy Manual, Section 4

### 4.1 Overview
This policy governs paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and unpaid leave of
absence for all full-time employees of The Organization. Part-time employees
accrue leave on a pro-rated basis under Section 4.6.

### 4.2 Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates based on years of
continuous service:
- Years 0–2: 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- Years 3–5: 20 days per year (1.667 days per month)
- Years 6+: 25 days per year (2.083 days per month)

PTO accrues monthly and may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 unused days
into the following calendar year. Any balance above 10 days on December 31
is forfeited unless a carryover exception has been approved in writing by a
department head.

### 4.3 Sick Leave
All employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year, credited in
full at the start of the year (not accrued monthly). Sick leave does not
carry over between years and has no cash-out value upon separation. Sick
leave may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or
to care for an immediate family member.

### 4.4 Requesting Leave
Leave requests for PTO must be submitted through the employee portal at
least 5 business days in advance for requests of 3 or more consecutive days,
and at least 1 business day in advance for shorter requests. Sick leave may
be reported on the day it is taken. All leave requests require approval from
the employee's direct supervisor and are subject to staffing-level review.

### 4.5 Leave of Absence (Unpaid)
Employees who have exhausted PTO and sick leave may request an unpaid leave
of absence of up to 12 weeks, consistent with applicable federal and state
family and medical leave laws. Unpaid leave requests require HR approval in
addition to supervisor approval and must include supporting documentation
where applicable (e.g., medical certification).

### 4.6 Part-Time Employees
Part-time employees accrue PTO and sick leave on a pro-rated basis
proportional to their regularly scheduled hours relative to a standard
40-hour work week. For example, an employee regularly scheduled for 20
hours per week accrues leave at 50% of the full-time rate listed above.

### 4.7 Holidays
The Organization observes 11 paid holidays per year, published annually in
the employee portal. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the
nearest business day as determined by HR.

### 4.8 Separation and Payout
Upon separation from employment, accrued and unused PTO (up to the 10-day
carryover cap) is paid out at the employee's final base rate of pay. Sick
leave is not paid out upon separation, consistent with Section 4.3.
